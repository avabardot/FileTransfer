# Pajama Same Back Story Idea

Pajama Sam is in their mid 40s. After achieving limited but aesthetically-destabalizing success young, pajama sam is moving home!   



Strangely enough, it was a phonecall from the neighbor complaining about the weeds that initially alerted Sam to the strange and temporally evasive disappearance of his parents. As any hero would, Sam lept into action, opting to give-up his life and return home in order to investigate their disappearance and most importantly, take care of the lawn.   



What we don't talk about, or what we definiely will not explore, is hidden within a calculation of loss - his loss - made when the idea of moving first crossed his mind. To say the least, it was not non-zero. To point this out though would make our hero into quite the anti-hero. Out of respect for him, we will call no attention. And if we were to really sit with ourselves a moment, who hasn't, at some point, hoped, if even for a moment, for some sort of disaster or tragedy, a call to action to deliver one from the present of now - to a different future? No, we won't mention again the hypnotic, subnautic desire Sam audibly recognized in himself for any sort of meaning to enter his life. We will not explore his mind's collapsing of the the thought or whether or not it simply buried a plane, missing the the vertex entirely. Sam and I will never tell you that it was so rooted, that upon receiving the phone call, a phone call that should have shaken him like jello in a windstorm, he only felt one thing - relief. Nope, it won't come up again because this story is about a hero.
